# Contributing to SoLeaf

> **SoLeaf does not accept outside contributions.**
> All SoLeaf repositories are proprietary to SoLeaf LLP and are maintained only by the SoLeaf team. Pull requests, patches and feature work from anyone outside the team are closed without review. Forking or copying a SoLeaf repository does not give you any right to use, run, modify or redistribute its code. See the repository's `LICENSE`.

If you are not a member of the SoLeaf team:

- **Found a security problem?** Report it privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.
- **Interested in licensing or a partnership?** Email [soleaf.co@gmail.com](mailto:soleaf.co@gmail.com).

## For SoLeaf team members

- Each repository has its own `CONTRIBUTING.md` with setup steps and conventions. Follow it where it exists.
- Work from a branch off the default branch (for example `feat/...`, `fix/...` or `docs/...`) and merge through a pull request.
- All work you do on SoLeaf repositories belongs to SoLeaf LLP.
- Do not copy SoLeaf code, data or documents into personal projects, other repositories or public places (gists, forums, AI tools that keep your data) without approval.
- Never commit secrets, `.env` files or real customer, farm or financial data.
